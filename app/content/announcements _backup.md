**2026-07-31 — New Landbird Density & Habitat Models are now available!**

This major update expands the data, geography, environmental information, and model-support products available for understanding the density, distribution, and habitat relationships of breeding landbirds.

Compared with the previous models, this version includes:

- **A broader geographic extent:** Canada, Alaska, and the hemiboreal Lower 48 United States, expanded from Canada alone
- **Far more observations:** approximately 1.45 million observations, increased from approximately 0.3 million and now including selected eBird records
- **More species:** 149 species, increased from 143
- **Updated predictions:** publicly available predictions representing 2020 conditions; predictions for 1990–2015 at five-year intervals are available upon request
- **Richer environmental information:** expanded and time-matched predictors describing vegetation biomass, human disturbance, annual climate, and other habitat conditions
- **Updated modelling regions:** 33 regions based on Bird Conservation Regions, with country boundaries and regional divisions used where appropriate
- **Improved predictive performance:** greater predictive accuracy when evaluated using observations withheld from model training
- **More information about model reliability:** cross-validated performance metrics, bootstrap standard deviation, species detection distance, environmental extrapolation, and training- and testing-sample density maps

Open **Explore Models → Landbird Density & Habitat** to view species profiles, maps, population and density estimates, environmental relationships, reliability information, and downloadable results. See **Use Models** for additional access options and **Methods** for details about the methods and workflow.
