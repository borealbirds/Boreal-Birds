The Boreal Avian Modelling Centre (BAM) combines bird observations from across North America with environmental information to develop high-resolution, large-scale predictive models. These products help connect ecological knowledge with practical decisions about species conservation, land-use planning, status assessment, and recovery.

This dashboard is the central place to discover and explore BAM model products. It brings model predictions together with species profiles, population and density estimates, environmental relationships, uncertainty and model-support information, methods, and guidance for accessing and using the models.

### Explore models

Open **Explore Models** to examine the products currently available in the dashboard:

- **Landbird Density & Habitat** presents BAM's current Version 5.0 models, including 2020 density predictions for 149 species across Canada, Alaska, and the hemiboreal Lower 48 United States.
- **Landbird Density & Habitat (v4 archive)** provides continued access to the previous model version.
- **Other Model Products** opens the BAM Geoportal, where you can discover additional spatial products and outputs from BAM research.

Model predictions represent expected conditions rather than direct observations. Consult the **Methods** page for information about model inputs, calibration, uncertainty, validation, reliability, limitations, and appropriate interpretation.

### Use models

Open **Use Models** to choose the workflow that best suits your needs. The [`BAMexploreR` R package](https://github.com/borealbirds/BAMexploreR) supports scripted access and analysis, including downloading prediction rasters, working with custom areas of interest, estimating population size, examining distributions, and exploring habitat relationships. A companion Shiny app provides a graphical interface, while the Google Earth Engine viewer supports interactive exploration over Google imagery.

The **Citation** page provides the publication and model-version references needed when using BAM results.

### Work with BAM

BAM also collaborates with partners to develop model applications and decision-support tools for specialized conservation and management needs. Visit **Contact BAM** to ask a question, discuss an application, or learn more about working with us.
