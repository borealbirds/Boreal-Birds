Models were fitted within **33 modelling regions based on Bird Conservation Regions (BCRs)**. Some BCRs were divided into multiple modelling regions to represent expected regional differences in habitat selection.

The study area includes Canada, Alaska, and the hemiboreal portion of the contiguous United States. Training datasets included observations within each modelling region and a 100-km buffer. Buffers did not cross the Canada–United States boundary because the environmental predictors available for the two countries differed.

Regional predictions were combined into three broad-scale mosaics: Canada, Alaska, and the hemiboreal Lower 48 United States.
