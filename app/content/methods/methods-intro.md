The **BAM Landbird Density & Habitat Models (Version 5.0)** estimate breeding-season density, distribution, population size, and environmental relationships for 149 landbird species. The models integrate 1,451,330 bird observations collected from 1980 through 2022 with spatially explicit environmental predictors. Separate boosted regression tree models were developed for 33 modelling regions and combined to produce broad-scale prediction products.

The methods below describe Version 5.0. Public raster products represent 2020 conditions at a 1-km resolution and express density as the expected number of male birds per hectare. Predictions for 1990 through 2015 at five-year intervals are available upon request through the [BAM contact page](https://borealbirds.ca/contact/).

The Version 5.0 workflow builds on the generalized framework described by [Stralberg et al. (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1002/ecs2.70405), which was applied to the archived Version 4 models. Because the data, geography, predictors, tuning, prediction workflow, and distributed products have changed, the sections on this page should be used as the methods summary for Version 5.0. Code used to implement the methods described on this page is publicly available on [BAM's Github](https://github.com/borealbirds/LandbirdModelsV5).
