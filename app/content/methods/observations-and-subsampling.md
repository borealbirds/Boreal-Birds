The modelling dataset contained **1,451,330 bird observations from 1980 through 2022**. It included 1,107,572 records accessed through WildTrax and 343,758 eBird records, representing conventional point counts and automated recording unit surveys.

eBird inputs were restricted to complete, stationary checklists lasting no more than 10 minutes. Surveys from all sources were retained when they:

- Occurred from June 1 through July 15
- Began between one hour before and six hours after sunrise
- Had a positive survey duration

Version 5.0 provides public results for **149 species**.

To reduce the influence of spatially and temporally clustered sampling, observations were assigned to 2.5-km spatial cells. For each of 32 bootstrap samples, one observation was selected from every available cell-and-year combination. Each bootstrap contained **580,603 observations**. A species was modelled within a region when it had more than 30 detections in the first bootstrap sample for that region.
