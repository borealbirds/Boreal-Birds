Version 5.0 models breeding-season landbird density in relation to environmental conditions across Canada, Alaska, and the hemiboreal portion of the contiguous United States. The products support exploration of species distributions, regional population sizes, and modelled habitat relationships.

Predicted density is expressed as **male birds per hectare** on a 1-km grid. Population estimates therefore represent **male individuals**, not all adult birds.

Predictions were prepared at five-year intervals from 1990 through 2020. The publicly released products represent 2020 conditions; predictions for 1990 through 2015 are available upon request.

Version 5.0 products include:

- Mean predicted density and its bootstrap standard deviation
- Distance to supporting species detections
- Population and regional mean-density summaries
- Predictor importance and partial dependence relationships
- Environmental extrapolation and training- and testing-sample density rasters
