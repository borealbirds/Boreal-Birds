Population size was estimated by summing pixel-level density predictions within each region and converting each 1-km² pixel to 100 hectares. Estimates are reported in millions of **male individuals**. Regional mean density is reported as male birds per hectare. Both summaries use the median and 5th and 95th percentiles calculated directly from the 32 bootstrap estimates.

Version 5.0 provides two complementary summaries of environmental relationships:

- **Predictor importance** is the mean relative influence of each retained predictor across the 32 bootstrap models. It ranks predictor contributions but does not show the direction of a relationship or establish causation.
- **Partial dependence relationships** show the mean estimated association between a predictor and predicted density after averaging over the other predictors. Curves show the mean across bootstrap models, with the 5th and 95th percentiles of the bootstrap distribution.

Partial dependence relationships are modelled associations and should not be interpreted as causal effects. Version 4 land-cover-based density summaries were discontinued because the expanded Version 5.0 predictor set provides more informative descriptions of environmental relationships.
