The Version 5.0 candidate library contained **103 environmental predictors** describing vegetation composition and biomass, climate, wetlands, land cover, human disturbance, roads, topography, and vegetation green-up.

Many environmental characteristics were represented at two spatial scales: a local 1-km value and a broader landscape value summarized over a 5 × 5-cell neighbourhood. The candidate predictors available to an individual modelling region varied with geographic coverage and ranged from 31 to 60 before subsequent model-based thinning.

Where time-varying environmental data were available, bird observations were matched to the nearest available covariate year or to the preceding covariate year, as appropriate for the source product. Static predictors were not temporally matched. Predictor-specific definitions, sources, spatial resolutions, available years, and temporal-matching rules are provided in the Version 5.0 results workbook.
