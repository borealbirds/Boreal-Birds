BAM has produced a range of model products to support bird conservation, research, and land-use planning. The resources below include archived density and abundance models, climate projections, habitat-suitability maps, and conservation-priority analyses.

Each product is hosted in a permanent or project-specific repository. Use the **Publication** link to learn about the methods and interpretation of a product, and the **Access the product** link to view or download the available maps and spatial data.

## Density and abundance models

These products describe bird distributions and how abundance varies across space or time. Note BAM is currently in the process of creating a new version of waterbird models, including waterfowl, shorebirds, and marsh birds.

### Landbird Density & Habitat Models — Archived Version 4

**Landbirds · Density and abundance · Habitat relationships · Canada · Archived**

The previous generation of BAM's landbird models provides predicted density, population estimates, and regional habitat relationships for 143 landbird species across Canada. These archived results remain available for users who need to reproduce earlier analyses or compare model versions.

- **[Explore the model results](https://borealbirds.github.io/)**
- **[Publication](https://doi.org/10.1002/ecs2.70405)**
- **[Access the dataset](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.4018335)**

### Spatiotemporal Abundance Maps of Breeding Waterfowl across Canada

**Waterfowl · Density and abundance · Spatiotemporal · Canada · 1990–2015**

Species-specific spatiotemporal abundance estimates for 18 waterfowl species across Canada. The models use annual species counts from 2,227 aerial-survey segments collected over 25 years from 1990 through 2015. Available spatial products include recorded and predicted total indicated pairs, Gaussian Markov random fields, and conditional predictive ordinates.

- **[Publication](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/epdf/10.1111/ddi.13129)**
- **[Access the product](https://figshare.com/s/f9f45a6198c4b35c7910)**

### Near-National Waterfowl Density and Abundance Maps for Canada

**Waterfowl · Density and abundance · Canada · Model uncertainty**

Predicted density and abundance maps for 13 waterfowl species and four species groups: goldeneyes, mergansers, scoters, and scaup. The products were developed with boosted regression trees using Waterfowl Breeding Population and Habitat Survey observations and environmental data. They also include nesting-guild maps, total waterfowl abundance, and species-specific model uncertainty.

- **[Publication](http://www.ace-eco.org/vol9/iss2/art7/)**
- **[Access the product](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1GWmc-QnGoQ-VVpsm9j5X675sgZO4_CEG)**

## Climate change projections

These products examine how bird distributions, abundance, or climate suitability may change under future climate conditions.

### Current and Projected Breeding-Waterfowl Distribution and Abundance in Eastern Canada

**Waterfowl · Climate projections · Eastern Canada · 1996–2100**

Baseline maps for 1996–2017 and projected maps for 2011–2100 showing predicted mean indicated breeding pairs and standard deviation for 12 waterfowl species in eastern Canada. Future results are summarized for three 30-year periods under lower (RCP4.5) and higher (RCP8.5) greenhouse-gas concentration trajectories. The products also include climate-suitability indices under four time-lag schemes.

- **[Publication](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10584-020-02829-9)**
- **[Access the product](https://figshare.com/s/da193f7507245b6e74e0)**

### Current and Projected Boreal Bird Densities

**Landbirds · Climate projections · Current and future conditions · 4-km resolution**

Species-specific mean density projections and uncertainty estimates for 80 boreal-breeding songbird species at a 4-km resolution. Products represent a current baseline based on 1961–1990 climate and three future periods: 2011–2040, 2041–2070, and 2071–2100.

- **[Publication](https://esajournals.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1890/13-2289.1)**
- **[Access the product](https://zenodo.org/record/3266449)**

## Habitat and conservation planning

These products describe relative habitat suitability or identify areas with high conservation value.

### Habitat-Suitability Maps for Boreal-Breeding Passerines

**Landbirds · Habitat suitability · Current conditions · Boreal and hemiboreal regions**

Species-specific maps of relative habitat suitability based on current climate and land cover. Values rank each grid cell relative to all cells in the boreal and hemiboreal study region: higher values indicate higher modelled habitat suitability for the species.

- **[Access the product](https://zenodo.org/record/3847272)**

### Priority Areas for Boreal Songbird Conservation in Canada

**Landbirds · Conservation planning · Priority areas · Canada**

Conservation-priority maps derived from 128 scenarios for 63 boreal passerine species. The scenarios were developed using the conservation-planning software Zonation and represent alternative approaches to identifying priority areas for boreal songbird conservation in Canada.

- **[Publication](https://www.ace-eco.org/vol13/iss2/art12/)**
- **[Access the product](https://zenodo.org/record/1491939)**
